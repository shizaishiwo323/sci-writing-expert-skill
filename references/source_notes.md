# Source Notes and Transformation Policy

## Primary pedagogical source

Hilary Glasman-Deal, *Science Research Writing for Non-Native Speakers of English*, Imperial College Press, 2010.

The book teaches writers to examine representative research articles, identify conventional section structures, grammar, and functional vocabulary, and apply those patterns to their own work.

## High-level concepts adapted

This Skill independently restates and operationalizes general concepts including:

- narrowing Introductions and widening Discussions;
- functional move models for Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion/Conclusion, Abstract, and Title;
- tense as a signal of rhetorical function;
- active/passive choices and agency;
- sentence connection and paragraph organization;
- articles, modifiers, sequence, frequency, quantity, causality, and modality;
- use of representative target articles to adapt generic conventions.

## Sentence corpus and exemplar papers

`references/corpus/` holds records and rules distilled from seven research papers, from the author's revision history, and from two exemplar papers:

- Niu, Q., and Zhang, C. (2018), *Geophysical Journal International*, 212, 1791-1805, doi:10.1093/gji/ggx501.
- Niu, Q., Zhang, C., and Prasad, M. (2020), *Journal of Geophysical Research: Solid Earth*, 125, e2020JB020515.

Paper IDs and source titles are listed in `references/corpus/extraction_protocol.md`. The corpus stores functional patterns, reasons, and source pointers. It does not store paper text. Records for the two exemplar papers add a sentence-by-sentence description of what each sentence does, with page pointers and short connector frames only.

## Copyright policy

The book PDF at the repository root (`Science Research Writing_副本2.pdf`) is the one allowed source document. It may be consulted when the references do not settle a question. Do not add other source PDFs, extracted book text, screenshots, exercises, answer keys, or extended examples to this repository.

When expanding the Skill:

1. extract the underlying general principle;
2. rewrite it independently;
3. add an operational decision rule or original example;
4. cite the bibliographic source here when appropriate;
5. verify that no copyrighted source file other than the book PDF is committed.
