# Notice

This repository contains an independently written, transformative scientific-writing workflow.

The design was informed in part by the pedagogical framework in:

Hilary Glasman-Deal, *Science Research Writing for Non-Native Speakers of English*, Imperial College Press, 2010.

A PDF copy of the book is kept at the repository root as `Science Research Writing_副本2.pdf`, at the repository owner's choice, so that the original can be consulted when needed. No other copy of its chapter text, exercises, answer keys, or extended examples is included in the Skill materials. The materials here paraphrase general writing principles, reorganize them into a Codex workflow, and add original routing, integrity checks, Chinese-to-English translation guidance, manuscript-audit logic, target-corpus adaptation, and evaluation cases.

The `references/corpus/` materials record general writing patterns observed in published papers and identify those papers by citation. They do not include the papers themselves. Copyright in those papers remains with their respective rightsholders.
